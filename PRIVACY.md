# VibeZip Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026.

VibeZip processes archives and previews on your Mac. The application does not
collect or transmit your files, filenames, archive passwords, browsing history
or usage data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and
its App Sandbox configuration disables incoming and outgoing network access.

## Files and folder access

You choose the files and folders VibeZip can access. To remember folder access,
the app stores macOS security-scoped bookmarks and folder paths locally, together
with the last authorized directory you visited. Preferences and window layout
also remain on your Mac.

Settings → Folder Access lets you add, renew or remove saved folder permissions.
Removing a saved permission does not delete your files. Access granted to an
overlapping folder may still cover the same location.

## Passwords and temporary files

Archive passwords are kept in memory. You can clear them from the Tools menu or
by quitting VibeZip. They are not saved as an app preference or sent to a server.

Previews and nested archives may require temporary copies in the app's temporary
storage. These copies can contain decrypted content. Normal cleanup removes
unused copies, but an interrupted operation or app termination can leave files
until a later cleanup or macOS removes them. Settings → General shows temporary
storage usage and lets you view the folder or clean unused files. Files in use
by open archives or running operations are kept.

Optional recovery copies are off by default. If enabled, a copy of the original
outer archive is retained in temporary storage after a successful edit. These
copies are included in temporary-file cleanup and may also be removed by macOS;
they are not permanent backups. Archive edits also use staging files beside the
original archive, which are separate from the temporary-folder cleanup control.
Deleting temporary files is not a secure-erasure guarantee.

## External services and support

The GitHub button opens the project page in your web browser. Finder actions use
macOS Finder. Archive previews stay inside VibeZip, and the app does not execute
archive contents or send them to external viewers or editors.

If you contact **[mac-service@vibezip.org](mailto:mac-service@vibezip.org)** or
submit a GitHub issue, you choose what information to share outside the app.
Support correspondence is used to respond to your request. GitHub issues are
public. Email services, GitHub and Apple operate under their own privacy policies;
the app's local processing does not prevent those services from handling data you
provide to them directly. Do not include archive passwords or confidential files
in support requests. You can contact the same email address about privacy or
request deletion of information you supplied through support.

## 中文隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日。

VibeZip 在你的 Mac 上处理压缩包和预览内容。应用不会收集或传输文件、文件名、
压缩包密码、浏览记录或使用数据；没有账户、分析统计、广告或跟踪 SDK。
应用的沙箱配置禁用了传入和传出网络访问。

### 文件与访问权限

由你选择允许访问的文件和文件夹。为记住授权，VibeZip 会在本机保存 macOS
安全作用域书签、文件夹路径及最后访问的已授权目录。偏好设置和窗口布局也保存在本机。

可在“设置 → 文件夹访问”中新增、更新或移除保存的权限。移除权限不会删除文件；
其他已授权的父文件夹仍可能允许访问相同位置。

### 密码与临时文件

压缩包密码仅保存在内存中，可通过“工具”菜单或退出应用清除，不会存入偏好设置或上传服务器。

预览和嵌套压缩包操作可能生成临时副本，其中可能包含解密后的内容。正常清理会移除不再
使用的副本；操作中断或应用退出异常可能留下文件，等待后续清理或 macOS 移除。
“设置 → 通用”可以查看临时文件占用、打开目录和清理未使用的文件，正在使用的文件会保留。

可选恢复副本默认关闭。启用后，成功编辑压缩包会在临时目录保留最外层原始压缩包的副本。
这些副本也会被临时文件清理或 macOS 移除，不属于永久备份。编辑时在原压缩包旁创建的
暂存文件与此临时目录不同，不由该清理按钮管理。删除临时文件不等同于安全擦除。

### 外部服务与联系

GitHub 按钮会通过系统浏览器打开项目页面；Finder 相关操作交由 macOS Finder 处理。
预览始终在 VibeZip 内进行，应用不会执行压缩包内容或将其交给外部查看器、编辑器。

联系 **[mac-service@vibezip.org](mailto:mac-service@vibezip.org)** 或提交 GitHub Issue
时，你自行决定在应用之外分享哪些信息。客服通信内容用于回复你的请求；GitHub Issue
为公开内容。邮件服务、GitHub 和 Apple 分别适用其自身的隐私政策。请勿发送压缩包密码
或机密文件。隐私问题以及对已向客服提供信息的删除请求，也可发送至该邮箱。

本政策的更新会发布在此页面，并标注新的生效日期。
